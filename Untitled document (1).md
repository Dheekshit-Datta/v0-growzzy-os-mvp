# **4\. WhatsApp Auto-Greeting**

 

**This is your final qualification layer. The video filters by emotion and revenue. The targeting filters by behavior and interest. The greeting filters by stated budget before you spend a single minute on a call.**

 

Set this as your auto-greeting in WhatsApp Business:

 

Hey\! We work with businesses spending ₹50K+ on marketing who want better results. Our retainers start at ₹65K/month. If that fits, tell us about your business and what you want to scale.

 

This greeting does three things:

•       **States who you serve:** businesses spending ₹50K+ on marketing. The ₹25K person reads this and does not reply.

•       **States your price:** ₹65K/month retainer. No ambiguity. No price shock on the call.

•       **Asks them to self-identify:** "tell us about your business" makes them invest effort before you invest time.

# **6\. Week-by-Week Optimization**

 

## **Week 1: Launch \+ Learn (Days 1-7)**

•       Launch with all 3 primary text variations in the single ad set.

•       Daily budget: ₹1,000/day.

•       Do NOT touch anything for 4 full days. Let Meta exit learning phase.

•       Monitor daily: Check every WhatsApp conversation. Score as Hot / Warm / Cold.

•       Respond to every lead within 5 minutes. Speed to lead is everything.

 

## **Week 2: First Cut (Days 8-14)**

•       Check which primary text variation has the lowest CPL \+ highest qualified lead rate.

•       Pause the worst-performing variation.

•       Add 1 new primary text variation to test.

•       Check frequency. If above 2.5, your audience is fatiguing. Consider adding secondary cities.

 

## **Week 3: Optimize (Days 15-21)**

•       If CPL is below ₹80 and qualified rate is above 25%, increase budget by 20% to ₹1,200/day.

•       Start building a Custom Audience: "People who messaged on WhatsApp in last 30 days." You will use this for Lookalike in Month 2\.

•       Test a static image ad alongside the video to catch different scroll behavior.

 

## **Week 4: Review \+ Plan Month 2 (Days 22-30)**

•       Full performance review. Calculate: Total conversations, qualified leads, cost per qualified lead, calls booked, deals closed.

•       If you have 50+ WhatsApp conversations, create a 1% Lookalike audience for Month 2\.

•       Plan Month 2: New primary text angles, possibly a second video creative, Lookalike ad set.

 

 

# **7\. KPI Targets**

 

| Metric | Target | Red Flag |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **CPL (WhatsApp conversation)** | ₹60-100 | Above ₹150 \= targeting too broad or creative fatigue |
| **CTR (link clicks)** | Above 1.0% | Below 0.8% \= hook is not stopping the scroll |
| **Qualified lead rate** | 25-35% | Below 15% \= targeting or greeting not filtering enough |
| **Cost per qualified lead** | ₹250-400 | Above ₹500 \= re-evaluate full funnel |
| **Frequency** | Below 2.5/week | Above 3.0 \= audience fatigue, expand cities |
| **Lead to call rate** | 40-50% | Below 30% \= follow-up speed or script issue |
| **Conversations/day** | 12-16 | Below 8 \= budget too low or audience too narrow |
